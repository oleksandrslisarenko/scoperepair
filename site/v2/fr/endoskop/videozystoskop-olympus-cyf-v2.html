Description

Description disponible en anglais.

Olympus CYF-V2 – High-quality flexible cystoscope for precise urological diagnostics

The Olympus CYF-V2 is a flexible video cystoscope that has been specially developed for precise urological examinations. It delivers high-quality images with lifelike reproduction of tissue structures, enabling reliable detection of changes in the mucous membrane. This makes the device ideal for diagnostic and therapeutic procedures in the urinary tract.

Optimal field of view for reliable orientation

With a 120° field of view, the Olympus CYF-V2 offers an excellent overview of the inside of the bladder. The upward and downward angulation of up to 210° enables precise navigation and facilitates the examination of sensitive areas such as the bladder neck. This means that even areas that are difficult to access can be reached safely.

High flexibility and comfortable handling

Thanks to its flexible design and ergonomic controls, the CYF-V2 is particularly comfortable to use. Precise control of the distal end supports controlled guidance of the cystoscope throughout the entire examination.

Versatile applications in urology

The Olympus CYF-V2 cystoscope is suitable for a wide range of urological applications, including diagnostic bladder examinations, therapeutic procedures, and examinations of the upper urinary tract. Its reliable image quality and flexibility make it a proven solution for use in clinics and practices.
