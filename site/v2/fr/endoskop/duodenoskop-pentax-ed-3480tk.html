Description

Description disponible en anglais.

Pentax ED-3480TK – Video duodenoscope with outstanding image quality

The Pentax ED-3480TK is a high-performance video duodenoscope designed for diagnostic and therapeutic procedures involving the bile and pancreatic ducts. Thanks to its high-resolution image sensors and excellent illumination, it provides clear, detailed views and outstanding image sharpness for precise endoscopic examinations.

High-resolution image display

The advanced imaging technology of the Pentax ED-3480TK enables sharp, high-contrast, and detailed visualization of mucosal structures and anatomical features. This supports reliable diagnostics and the safe performance of interventional procedures.

Optimal illumination for clear visibility

The powerful illumination produces bright, evenly lit images. This improves orientation within the examination area and facilitates the assessment of tissues and structures.

Large working channel for therapeutic applications

The generously sized working channel allows for the use of various instruments for diagnostic and therapeutic procedures. This makes the Pentax ED-3480TK ideal for ERCP applications and complex interventional procedures.

Designed for demanding ERCP procedures

The duodenoscope supports the precise performance of endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography (ERCP) and offers the necessary flexibility for modern therapeutic applications.

Ideal for interventional endoscopy

With its combination of outstanding image quality, reliable performance, and therapeutic versatility, the Pentax ED-3480TK is a high-performance solution for modern biliary and pancreatic endoscopy.
