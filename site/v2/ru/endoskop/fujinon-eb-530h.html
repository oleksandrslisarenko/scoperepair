Описание

Описание доступно на английском.

Fujinon EB-530H – High-resolution video bronchoscope for precise diagnostics

The Fujinon EB-530H delivers clear, high-resolution images that enable precise visualization of the airways. The finest mucosal structures and vascular patterns are clearly visible, supporting reliable assessment during bronchoscopy.

Optimized field of view With a wide field of view, the EB-530H provides a comprehensive overview of the trachea and bronchi. This allows lesions and anatomical structures to be reliably identified and assessed.

Ergonomic handling and precise control The flexible angulation of the distal tip enables safe navigation even in hard-to-reach areas of the respiratory tract. Ergonomic operation ensures controlled and comfortable use during diagnostic and therapeutic procedures.

Versatile working channel The integrated working channel allows the use of biopsy forceps, brushes, and suction accessories. This makes the Fujinon EB-530H suitable for both diagnostic and therapeutic bronchoscopy applications.

Reliable integration into existing systems The Fujinon EB-530H is compatible with the corresponding Fujinon video processors and can be easily integrated into existing endoscopy workflows. Its robust design ensures a long service life in everyday clinical use.
