Описание

Описание доступно на английском.

Fujinon EC-600WM – High-resolution image quality, close focus, and FICE

The Fujinon EC-600WM is a modern video duodenoscope for precise diagnostic and therapeutic procedures. Equipped with a megapixel CMOS image sensor, it delivers clear, bright, high-resolution images with reduced image noise. The advanced CMOS technology ensures a detailed display of even the finest mucosal structures.

Excellent image quality and stable display

Thanks to the integrated anti-blur function, the sharpest still images are automatically selected. This supports reliable documentation and ensures optimally focused images at all times during the examination.

Optimal illumination in close-up and distant ranges

Thanks to automatic photometric control, the Fujinon EC-600WM produces evenly illuminated images in both close-up and distant ranges. This ensures that contrasts and details remain clearly visible regardless of the working distance.

FICE technology for improved mucosal assessment

With advanced FICE (Flexible Spectral Imaging Color Enhancement) imaging, the EC-600WM offers even clearer and higher-contrast images than previous models. This allows for better differentiation between normal mucosa and pathological changes such as lesions or inflammatory areas.

Close focus for precise detailed diagnostics

The integrated close focus mode up to 2 mm allows for particularly detailed examination of the mucosa and supports precise assessment of even the smallest structures. This significantly increases diagnostic reliability in demanding examinations.

Clinical benefits

The Fujinon EC-600WM combines high resolution, modern image processing, FICE technology, and close focus in one powerful system. It is ideal for users who value reliable image quality, improved lesion detection, and precise diagnostics in ERCP and special examinations.
