Fujinon EG-590WR – Excellent Image Quality and FICE

The Fujinon EG-590WR impresses with its outstanding image quality and natural color reproduction. Thanks to proven Super CCD technology, the gastroscope delivers sharp, high-contrast, and highly detailed images that support precise assessment of the mucosa during the examination.

Excellent image quality with Super CCD

The powerful Super CCD image sensor ensures high-quality image reproduction with natural colors and high detail accuracy. This clearly displays mucosal structures and reliably highlights diagnostic abnormalities.

FICE for improved tissue visualization

With integrated FICE (Flexible Spectral Imaging Color Enhancement) technology, mucosal structures and vascular patterns can be visualized with greater contrast. This aids in the detection and assessment of suspicious tissue changes during the examination.

Slim design for comfortable examinations

With an introducer tube measuring just 9.3 mm in diameter, the Fujinon EG-590WR combines a slim design with high performance. This enables comfortable handling without sacrificing important functions for daily diagnostics.

Ideal for routine examinations

The Fujinon EG-590WR was developed for reliable use in daily gastroenterology. The combination of high image quality, natural color reproduction, and FICE technology makes it a powerful solution for routine and follow-up examinations.
