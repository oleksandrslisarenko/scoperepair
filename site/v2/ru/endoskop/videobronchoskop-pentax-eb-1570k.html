Pentax EB-1570K – High-quality video bronchoscope for respiratory diagnostics

The Pentax EB-1570K is a flexible video bronchoscope designed specifically for the examination and treatment of the respiratory tract. With its integrated image sensor, it delivers clear, high-quality images that support precise visualization of the tracheobronchial tree – ideal for diagnostic and therapeutic procedures.

Excellent image quality and field of view

The EB-1570K has a 120° field of view, providing a comprehensive overview of the respiratory tract and facilitating the detection of lesions and structures. The optical quality ensures a detailed representation of the environment and supports safe clinical assessment.

Ergonomic handling and efficient control

The bronchoscope is equipped with ergonomically positioned suction valves and angulation levers that enable intuitive operation and precise control of the distal end. The exceptional angulation of the tip (up to 210° upward and 130° downward) facilitates navigation even in complex anatomical areas.

Practical instrument integration

With a working channel diameter of 2.0 mm, the EB-1570K can be easily combined with biopsy forceps and other accessories. This supports both diagnostic procedures such as biopsies and therapeutic measures during bronchoscopy.

Compatibility & reliability

The bronchoscope is compatible with Pentax EPK series video processors and many documentation systems, allowing for easy integration into existing endoscopy workflows. It is designed for repeated use and can be used safely after appropriate disinfection and sterilization.
