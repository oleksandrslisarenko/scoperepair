FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Your question isn’t listed? Call us: +49 4155 8237-331

What does an endoscopy system include?

An endoscopy system consists of all the components required for a professional endoscopic examination. These include, among others, a video processor, light source, monitor, flexible endoscopes, and the appropriate accessories. We're happy to put together a system tailored specifically to your requirements.

Are used endoscopy systems a reliable alternative to new devices?

Yes. All endoscopy systems are technically inspected and thoroughly tested before delivery. They offer an economical alternative to new devices while still meeting high quality standards.

Do I get a warranty on refurbished endoscopy systems?

Yes. You receive a 6-12 month warranty on our used endoscopy systems. This gives you added investment security and a reliable solution for your practice or clinic operations.

Can existing endoscopes be integrated into a new endoscopy system?

In many cases, yes. We check the compatibility of your existing endoscopes and system components, and advise you on the best way to integrate them into a new or existing endoscopy system.

Does scopeREPAIR support planning an endoscopy system?

Yes. We provide manufacturer-independent advice and work with you to develop a tailored solution — from selecting individual components to fully equipping your endoscopy unit.

Does scopeREPAIR also offer installation and training?

Yes. On request, we handle the professional installation, commissioning, and training of your medical staff, so your endoscopy system is ready for use quickly and safely.
