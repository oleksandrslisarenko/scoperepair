FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Your question isn’t listed? Call us: +49 4155 8237-331

Which endoscopes does scopeREPAIR purchase?

We purchase used flexible endoscopes and endoscopy equipment from various manufacturers. This includes, in particular, devices from Olympus, Pentax, Fujinon, and Storz, as well as matching components such as video processors, light sources, and accessories.

Does scopeREPAIR also buy defective or no longer functional endoscopes?

Yes. Defective, older, or incomplete endoscopes can also be of interest to us. We assess each device individually, taking into account its condition, model, repair effort, and existing components.

What devices can I sell to scopeREPAIR?

In addition to flexible endoscopes, we also purchase other endoscopy equipment components, such as video processors, light sources, monitors, endoscopy carts, suction pumps, and accessories.

How is the value of my used endoscope determined?

The valuation is based on various factors such as manufacturer, model, technical condition, age, equipment, accessories, and any refurbishment effort required. This allows us to realistically assess the true market value of your device.

Can I sell multiple endoscopes or an entire equipment inventory?

Yes. If you'd like to sell multiple devices or an entire endoscopy inventory, we're happy to review your full equipment. For larger inventories, an on-site assessment can be arranged.

Can I trade in my old endoscope for another device?

Yes. As part of modernizing your equipment, we also offer trade-in options. This lets you factor in the value of your existing medical equipment when purchasing a refurbished device.
