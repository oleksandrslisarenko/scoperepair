What is a refurbished endoscope?

A refurbished endoscope has previously been used in medical practice and was then thoroughly inspected, repaired, and technically reworked by our technicians. Worn parts are replaced, and every device is carefully tested before sale.

Are scopeREPAIR's used endoscopes safe and tested?

Yes. All the used endoscopes we offer go through a multi-stage refurbishment process. Every device is technically and visually overhauled, and thoroughly tested for function and operational readiness.

Do I get a warranty on used endoscopes?

Yes. You receive a 12-month warranty on all refurbished endoscopes. This gives practices and clinics added investment security when purchasing refurbished medical equipment.

Can used endoscopes be integrated into existing practice systems?
