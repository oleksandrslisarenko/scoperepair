Description

Fujinon EG-530D – Excellent image quality and two working channels

The Fujinon EG-530D impresses with its outstanding image quality and natural color reproduction. Thanks to its powerful imaging technology, the gastroscope delivers clear, high-contrast, and highly detailed images that enable a precise assessment of the mucosa during the examination.

Excellent image display

The high-quality optics ensure sharp, detailed images with natural color reproduction. This allows for precise visualization of mucosal structures and supports reliable diagnostic assessment.

Two working channels for expanded therapeutic options

The Fujinon EG-530D features two working channels with diameters of 3.8 mm and 2.8 mm. This design allows for the simultaneous use of different instruments and expands the possibilities for diagnostic and therapeutic procedures.

Wide field of view for optimal overview

With a wide 140° field of view, the gastroscope provides an excellent overview of the examination area. This allows larger mucosal areas to be captured and efficiently assessed.

Ideal for diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy

The combination of excellent image quality, natural color reproduction, two working channels, and a wide field of view makes the Fujinon EG-530D a high-performance solution for demanding gastroenterological examinations and interventions.
