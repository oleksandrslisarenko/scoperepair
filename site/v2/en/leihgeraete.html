Frequently asked questions

Can I get a loaner device during the repair?

Yes. To minimize downtime in your clinic or practice, we provide compatible loaner endoscopes during the repair. Availability depends on the specific model.

Does scopeREPAIR provide loaner devices during repair?

Yes. To keep downtime and disruption in your practice or hospital to a minimum, we provide a suitable replacement device from our loaner service during the repair, subject to availability.
