Pentax ED34-i10T2 – Duodenoscope with outstanding HD+ image quality

The Pentax ED34-i10T2 duodenoscope features advanced HD+ imaging technology and delivers outstanding image quality for precise endoscopic examinations. It supports both standard procedures and challenging endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography (ERCP) with retrograde access, thereby facilitating complex therapeutic interventions.

High-resolution HD+ imaging

Thanks to its powerful HD+ resolution, the Pentax ED34-i10T2 offers sharp, high-contrast, and highly detailed visualization of the mucosa. Even fine mucosal structures are clearly displayed, supporting precise diagnostic assessment.

Optimal visualization of mucosal details

The improved image quality enables an accurate assessment of the mucosa and helps reliably identify and classify pathological changes.

Support for ERCP procedures

The Pentax ED34-i10T2 is specifically designed for interventional endoscopic procedures. It facilitates the performance of ERCP procedures while providing reliable visual control of the examination area.

Ideal for diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy

With its combination of HD+ image quality, precise detail rendering, and suitability for interventional procedures, the Pentax ED34-i10T2 is a high-performance solution for modern gastroenterological applications.
