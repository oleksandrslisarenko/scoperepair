Pentax EG-1690K – Transnasal video gastroscope with outstanding image quality

The Pentax EG-1690K is a high-performance transnasal video gastroscope that impresses with its outstanding image quality and sleek design. Thanks to advanced image sensors and optimized illumination, the gastroscope delivers clear, detailed views and supports precise diagnostic examinations of the upper gastrointestinal tract.

High-resolution and clear image display

The Pentax EG-1690K’s high-performance image sensors ensure high-resolution, high-contrast, and highly detailed visualization of mucosal structures. Even fine mucosal details are rendered with precision, facilitating diagnostic assessment.

Optimal illumination for precise diagnostics

Thanks to the excellent illumination, you obtain bright and sharp images with high detail during the examination. This supports the reliable detection of mucosal changes and anatomical structures.

Transnasal design for greater patient comfort

The transnasal design of the Pentax EG-1690K enables a particularly gentle and comfortable examination. The slim insertion tube improves patient tolerance and supports a comfortable gastroscopy procedure.

Ideal for everyday clinical practice

With its combination of high image quality, precise visualization, and comfortable handling, the Pentax EG-1690K is ideally suited for routine and specialized examinations in gastroenterology.
