Olympus PCF-H180AL – Precise HDTV image quality in a slim design

The Olympus PCF-H180AL impresses with high-resolution HDTV imaging and a slim design that enables comfortable and precise colonoscopy. Even the finest mucosal structures are clearly displayed, supporting reliable diagnosis.

High-resolution image quality and wide depth of field

Thanks to its HDTV image sensor, the PCF-H180AL delivers razor-sharp images. The wide depth of field of 2–100 mm enables detailed visualization of even the smallest mucosal changes and ensures precise assessment during the examination.

NBI for improved contrast imaging

The NBI (Narrow Band Imaging) mode provides higher-contrast visualization of mucosal surfaces and vascular structures. This facilitates the detection of lesions and supports differentiated diagnostics.

Stiffness Control for optimal guidance

The integrated Stiffness Control allows flexible adjustment of the insertion tube to the anatomical conditions of the colon. This facilitates navigation and increases examination comfort.

Close Focus for precise detail analysis

With the Close Focus function, the endoscope tip can be brought as close as 2 mm to the mucosa. Without electronic magnification, the image remains sharp and rich in detail thanks to HDTV, enabling an accurate assessment of the mucosa and capillaries.

Optimized for modern colonoscopy

The combination of HDTV technology, NBI, Close Focus, and Stiffness Control makes the Olympus PCF-H180AL a powerful solution for precise and safe endoscopic examinations in hospitals and clinics.
