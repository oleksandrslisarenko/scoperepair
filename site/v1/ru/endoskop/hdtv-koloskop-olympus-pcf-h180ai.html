Описание

Описание доступно на английском.

Olympus PCF-H180Ai – Precise HDTV image quality in a slim design

The Olympus PCF-H180Ai delivers high-resolution HDTV imaging and features a slim design for comfortable and precise colonoscopy. Even the finest mucosal structures are clearly visible, supporting reliable diagnosis.

High-resolution image quality and large depth of field

Equipped with an HDTV image sensor, the PCF-H180Ai delivers razor-sharp images. The large depth of field of 2–100 mm enables detailed visualization of even the smallest mucosal changes and ensures precise assessment during the examination.

NBI for high-contrast imaging

The NBI (Narrow Band Imaging) mode provides improved visualization of mucosal surfaces and vascular structures. This allows lesions to be detected earlier and assessed with greater precision.

Stiffness Control for optimal handling

With the integrated Stiffness Control, the insertion tube can be flexibly adapted to the anatomical conditions of the colon. This facilitates navigation and increases comfort during the colonoscopy.

Close Focus for precise detail analysis

The Close Focus function allows the endoscope tip to be brought as close as 2 mm to the mucosa. In combination with HDTV, the image remains clear, sharp, and rich in detail, enabling precise assessment of the mucosa and capillaries.

Efficient for modern endoscopy

Through the combination of HDTV technology, NBI, Close Focus, and Stiffness Control, the Olympus PCF-H180Ai offers a powerful solution for precise and safe endoscopic examinations in hospitals and clinics.
