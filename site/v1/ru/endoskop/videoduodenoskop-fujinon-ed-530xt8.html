Fujinon ED-530XT8 – Video Duodenoscope for Precise ERCP Applications

The Fujinon ED-530XT8 video duodenoscope offers excellent image quality and reliable visualization of anatomical structures in the upper gastrointestinal tract. Thanks to state-of-the-art optics, the system delivers clear, high-contrast, and highly detailed images, thereby supporting precise diagnostic and therapeutic procedures.

High-quality image display for reliable diagnostics

The high-performance imaging ensures a sharp and natural view of the mucosa and facilitates the assessment of the papilla, bile ducts, and pancreatic duct during the examination.

Optimized for ERCP procedures

The Fujinon ED-530XT8 is specifically designed for endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography (ERCP) and supports both diagnostic and therapeutic procedures in the biliary and pancreatic systems.

Ergonomic design for precise handling

The duodenoscope is designed for safe and controlled navigation within the examination area and supports the efficient performance of complex endoscopic procedures.

Ideal for interventional endoscopy

Combining excellent image quality, stable handling, and ERCP optimization, the Fujinon ED-530XT8 is a reliable solution for demanding duodenoscopic applications.
