Pentax EC-3890LK – Impressive image quality for precise examinations

The Pentax EC-3890LK impresses with its high-quality image display and, thanks to modern image sensors and optimized illumination, delivers clear, detailed views and outstanding image sharpness during endoscopic examinations.

High-resolution and detailed image display

The powerful image sensors of the Pentax EC-3890LK ensure a sharp, high-contrast, and precise display of mucosal structures. Even fine mucosal details are clearly visualized, supporting reliable diagnostics.

Optimal illumination for clear images

Thanks to the excellent illumination, you obtain bright, highly detailed images with high image sharpness during the examination. This facilitates the assessment of anatomical structures and mucosal changes.

Precise diagnostics in everyday clinical practice

The Pentax EC-3890LK is ideally suited for routine and demanding endoscopic examinations. The combination of clear image quality and reliable performance supports efficient and precise examination procedures.

Ideal for modern endoscopy

With its combination of high-resolution imaging, optimal illumination, and precise visualization, the Pentax EC-3890LK provides optimal conditions for reliable diagnostic applications.
