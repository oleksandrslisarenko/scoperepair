How the repair works

How an endoscope repair works

  1. Step 1 of 7

    Damage report / pick-up order

    Place a pick-up order directly or report the damage by phone or e-mail. For visible damage, a photo helps us. During the repair you can use our loaner service.

  2. Step 2 of 7

    Analysis

    Once the device arrives, we carry out a detailed damage analysis and check various control points. In some cases, partial disassembly is needed to identify all malfunctions.

  3. Step 3 of 7

    Cost estimatenon-binding

    After the analysis you receive a detailed, non-binding cost estimate. We answer all your questions so you can make an informed decision.

  4. Step 4 of 7

    Your approvalYou decide

    After receiving the estimate, we need your written approval by e-mail or fax. Only then does the repair begin.

  5. Step 5 of 7

    Repair

    Our technicians start as soon as possible. Subject to availability we provide a free loaner — you only pay shipping. We keep you updated on progress and expected completion.

  6. Step 6 of 7

    Final inspectionTest report

    After the repair, your endoscope undergoes a thorough final inspection. You receive the result with the device; it is also documented in our archive.

  7. Step 7 of 7

    Return shippingExpress

    We send your endoscope back by express. We arrange collection of any loaner with you, so you have time to test your device before its next use.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Your question isn’t listed? Call us: +49 4155 8237-331

How does an endoscope repair work?

After you report the damage, we can arrange to pick up your endoscope on request. A careful damage analysis follows, including a leak test of the device. You'll receive a transparent cost estimate. The repair is carried out to strict quality standards using validated testing procedures, followed by a final safety and function check before the device is shipped back.

How long does an endoscope repair take?

Repair time depends on the extent of the damage and the availability of the required spare parts. Once you give your approval, our technicians begin the repair as quickly as possible. Our goal is to keep the downtime of your endoscopes to a minimum.

Does scopeREPAIR offer a pickup service?

Yes. We arrange the pickup of your defective endoscope, so you don't need to handle the shipping yourself. Once the repair is complete, we reliably send your device back to you.

What warranty do I get on the repair?

You receive a 6-month warranty on all endoscope repairs we carry out. This covers the defects fixed as part of the repair.

Do I need to sign a maintenance contract?

No. A maintenance contract is not required for a repair. We only repair the components that are actually necessary — transparently, economically, and without hidden costs.

Do I receive documentation of the repair?

Yes. After the repair is complete, every endoscope undergoes a thorough final inspection. The test results are documented and handed over together with the device.

What happens if a repair is no longer worthwhile?

If a repair is not economically worthwhile, we'll advise you transparently on possible alternatives. On request, we can also offer you refurbished endoscopes with a 12-month warranty.

Which endoscopes does scopeREPAIR repair?

We repair flexible endoscopes from all common manufacturers. We specialize particularly in devices from Olympus, Pentax, Fujinon, and Storz. We also carry out repairs on endoscopes from other manufacturers.

Need help with a defect fast?

Call the workshop directly. We’ll sort out the next steps on the phone.
