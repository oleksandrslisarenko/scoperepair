Description

Fujinon EB-530T – High-Performance Video Bronchoscope for Precise Airway Diagnostics

The Fujinon EB-530T is a flexible video bronchoscope designed for diagnostic and therapeutic examination of the airways. Thanks to advanced CCD image sensor technology, the Fujinon EB-530T delivers clear, high-contrast images for reliable assessment of the tracheobronchial tree.

The high image quality supports precise visualization of mucosal structures and facilitates the detection of pathological changes during bronchoscopy.

High image quality and wide field of view

With its wide field of view, the Fujinon EB-530T provides a comprehensive overview of the airways. Its optical performance ensures a detailed image even in challenging anatomical areas and supports reliable diagnosis.

Precise control and ergonomic handling

The Fujinon EB-530T impresses with excellent articulation of the distal tip, allowing for safe navigation even in peripheral bronchial sections. The ergonomic design of the handle and logically placed controls ensure comfortable and intuitive operation during the examination.

Large working channel for versatile applications

With its generously sized working channel, the Fujinon EB-530T is ideally suited for the use of biopsy forceps, brushes, and suction accessories. This allows both diagnostic procedures and therapeutic measures to be performed efficiently.

Reliability and System Compatibility

The Fujinon EB-530T is compatible with the video systems of Fujinon endoscopy platforms and can be easily integrated into existing clinical workflows. Its robust construction ensures a long service life and reliable performance in everyday clinical practice.
