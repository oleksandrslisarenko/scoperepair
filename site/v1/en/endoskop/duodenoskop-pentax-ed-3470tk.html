Description

Pentax ED-3470TK – Video duodenoscope for precise ERCP procedures

The Pentax ED-3470TK is a high-performance video duodenoscope specifically designed for diagnostic and therapeutic procedures involving the bile and pancreatic ducts. Thanks to its high-resolution image sensors and excellent illumination, it provides clear, detailed views and outstanding image sharpness for demanding endoscopic procedures.

High-resolution image quality for precise diagnostics

The advanced imaging technology of the Pentax ED-3470TK enables sharp, high-contrast, and highly detailed visualization of mucosal structures and anatomical features. This makes even subtle changes reliably visible and allows for precise assessment.

Optimal illumination for clear visibility

The powerful lighting ensures bright and evenly illuminated images throughout the entire examination area. This improves orientation during the examination and supports the safe performance of complex procedures.

Large working channel for therapeutic interventions

The generously sized working channel allows for the use of a wide variety of instruments for diagnostic and therapeutic applications. This makes the Pentax ED-3470TK ideal for demanding ERCP procedures and interventional procedures.

Designed for modern ERCP procedures

The duodenoscope supports the precise performance of endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography (ERCP) and offers the necessary flexibility for complex biliary and pancreatic interventions.

Ideal for interventional endoscopy

With its combination of outstanding image quality, reliable performance, and therapeutic versatility, the Pentax ED-3470TK is a high-performance solution for modern duodenoscopy and ERCP.
