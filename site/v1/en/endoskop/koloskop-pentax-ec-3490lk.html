Description

Pentax EC-3490LK – Impressive image quality for precise examinations

The Pentax EC-3490LK delivers high-quality imaging and, thanks to modern image sensors and optimized illumination, provides clear, detailed views and outstanding image sharpness during endoscopic examinations. The precise visualization of mucosal structures supports reliable diagnostics in everyday clinical practice.

High-resolution image quality

The Pentax EC-3490LK’s high-performance image sensors enable sharp, high-contrast, and highly detailed visualization of mucosal surfaces. Even fine mucosal structures become clearly visible, facilitating the assessment of areas of abnormal tissue.

Optimal illumination for clear, detailed views

Thanks to the excellent illumination, you obtain bright and evenly lit images during the examination. This improves the view of anatomical structures and ensures high image quality throughout the entire examination.

Precise Diagnostics in Daily Practice

The combination of high resolution and excellent image sharpness supports reliable assessment of mucosal changes and contributes to the efficient performance of diagnostic examinations.

Reliable performance for modern endoscopy

With its combination of outstanding image quality, clear detail, and reliable performance, the Pentax EC-3490LK is ideal for routine examinations as well as demanding endoscopic applications.
