FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does scopeREPAIR stand for?

As a manufacturer-independent specialist, scopeREPAIR offers professional repair, maintenance, and distribution of flexible medical endoscopes. With years of experience and a quality management system certified to DIN EN ISO 9001:2015, we support hospitals, clinics, and medical practices with economical, sustainable, and cross-manufacturer solutions.

Which endoscopes does scopeREPAIR repair?

As a manufacturer-independent specialist, we repair and service flexible endoscopes from the leading brands Olympus, Pentax, and Fujinon. Our medical technology service covers all common endoscope types, including gastroscopes, colonoscopes, bronchoscopes, cystoscopes, and duodenoscopes.

Why does professional endoscope repair without a maintenance contract pay off?

Our philosophy is simple: necessary repairs instead of unnecessary fixed costs. Professional repair by our experienced technicians extends the lifespan of your medical equipment, reduces investment costs, and offers an economical alternative to long-term maintenance contracts. We only ever replace components that are actually defective.

Does scopeREPAIR also offer used endoscopes?

Our range includes professionally inspected, refurbished endoscopes as well as complete endoscopy systems from renowned manufacturers. For added investment security, you receive a 12-month warranty when purchasing a used endoscope.

Are original spare parts used for endoscope repairs?

For every endoscope repair, scopeREPAIR uses only high-quality spare parts. This ensures reliable function, high safety standards, and long-term use of your medical equipment.

How does scopeREPAIR's repair service work?

The process is simple and transparent: you can commission us via the pickup order form, or contact us directly for personal consultation. After a technical analysis, you'll receive a detailed cost estimate. Your endoscope is only repaired once you've given your approval.

Does scopeREPAIR provide loaner devices during repair?

Yes. To keep downtime and disruption in your practice or hospital to a minimum, we provide a suitable replacement device from our loaner service during the repair, subject to availability.
