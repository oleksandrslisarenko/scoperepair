Privacy policy

What and how we process data

On this page we inform you in what way, to what extent and for what purpose we collect personal data when you use this website. In no case your data will be passed on to third parties!

Respon­sibility

scopeREPAIR GmbH Raiffeisenstraße 11 21516 Müssen Germany

Purpose of storing personal data

When you use this website, data is transmitted from your webbrowser (Firefox, Internet Explorer, Chrome, Safari, etc.) to the webserver. On this accasion usually your IP address, connection date and time, URL of the page called, accessing client ID (browser version, operating system, plug-ins, etc.) and HTTP status of the request are saved. This data is collected for the server-operation of the website by the host-provider, for technical analysis and for evaluating the number of visitors. This data will be deleted by the host-provider after three months.

Your data will not be sold, rented or made available to third parties. Data-transfers based on governmental orders and laws are only made within the framework of national regulations.

Data processing by making contact

If you contact us by email, telephone, post or other media, data will be transmitted. This data will only be used for the purpose corresponding to your request. According to Art. 6 Para. 1 Clause 1 lit. a) GDPR, you give us your voluntary consent to processing by contacting us. You can revoke this consent at any time in accordance with Art. 7 Paragraph 3 GDPR.

Contact form

We use our contact form to collect your contact details such as your name, telephone number, email address and the reason for your request. These data are only stored and processed by our company for the purpose of establishing contact and processing your request.

SSL encryption

We attach great importance to the protection of your data, which is why we encrypt the data transport using SSL.

Legitimation of data processing

The data processing takes place in accordance with Art. 6 Para. 1 Clause 1 lit. f) GDPR for the above-mentioned purposes.

Google Maps

We use Google Maps to display maps on our website. This service is operated by Google (Google Inc., 1600 Amphitheater Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA). For more information on Google’s use of the data and their privacy policy visit: https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps.html

Affected rights

Right to information in accordance with Art. 15 GDPR

You can request information about your personal data we have collected and processed at any time. This includes the processing purpose, the data-category, the recipients and the categories of recipients, the duration of storage, the existence of a right to correction or deletion, the right to complain to authorities, origin of the data, existence of automated decision-making including profiling in accordance with Article 22 paragraphs 1 and 4

Right to correction in accordance with Art. 16 GDPR

You can ask us to correct incorrect or incomplete data, which we will of course carry out immediately.

Right to erasure in accordance with Art. 17 GDPR

You can request the deletion of your personal data, implied that this does not conflict with other interests in accordance with Paragraph 3. The right of erasure does not exist if the following reasons conflict: right to freedom of expression and information, fulfillment of legal obligations, reasons of public interest in the field of public health, archiving purposes in the public interest, scientific or historical purposes according to Art. 89 paragraph 1 or for the establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims.

Right to restriction of processing in accordance with Art. 18 GDPR

If you dispute the accuracy of the personal data, you can request that we restrict processing for the duration of the review. This right also exists if you refuse the deletion of the data, if this is required for the exercise or defense of legal claims or you have objected to the processing of the data, as long as it is not yet certain whether the legitimate reasons of the person responsible for the data processing outweigh you.

Right to transfer according to Art. 20 GDPR

You are entitled to receive your personal data in a structured, common and machine-readable format. You can also request that we transfer this data to a third party.

Right of objection in accordance with Art. 21 GDPR

According to Paragraph 1, you can revoke your consent to data processing at any time. You can also object to direct mail from us under Paragraph 2. For this purpose, an email to info@sr.ontola.de is sufficient
